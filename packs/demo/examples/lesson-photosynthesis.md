# Lesson: How Plants Make Food (Photosynthesis)

**Why this matters:** Almost every calorie you eat traces back to a plant
turning sunlight into sugar. Understand this and you understand the base of
nearly every food chain.

## The one idea
Plants take in carbon dioxide and water, and — using energy from sunlight —
convert them into glucose (a sugar) and oxygen.

## Worked example
A leaf in bright sun is a tiny factory:
1. Roots pull up **water**.
2. Tiny pores (stomata) let in **carbon dioxide**.
3. Chlorophyll (the green pigment) captures **sunlight**.
4. Out comes **glucose** (food) and **oxygen** (which we breathe).

## Recap
Sunlight + water + CO₂ → sugar + oxygen. If you remember the inputs and the
outputs, you remember photosynthesis.
